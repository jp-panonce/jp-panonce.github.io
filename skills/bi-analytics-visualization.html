What I Can Deliver

  • Interactive Tableau and Power BI dashboards for operational and executive audiences.
  • KPI definitions and reporting views aligned with business questions.
  • Exploratory analysis using Python, Pandas, R, SQL, MATLAB, and Excel.
  • Supply-chain visibility and data quality reporting based on warehouse data.
  • Repeatable data preparation and validation workflows for dependable reporting.
  • Clear documentation of calculations, filters, assumptions, and refresh requirements.

How I Work

Start with the decision. I clarify the audience, questions, measures, and actions the analysis should support.

Validate before visualizing. I inspect sources, definitions, and data quality before building the final view.

Design for interpretation. I organize dashboards around comparison, context, and exceptions rather than decoration.
