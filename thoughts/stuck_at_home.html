On Lockdown

"Bored in the housed, bored in the house, bored"

That's what a popular tiktok song says. It perfectly describes what many of us is probably feeling right now. COVID has forced us inside our homes. well, for a good cause which is to prevent further spread of the virus.

Here in Cebu, the government imposed a strict lockdown allowing only 2 (max) person per household to come out at a time. That means, I can only go out when buying food or other essentials. I can't even meet my friends. Thus, online is the only way to interact with them. :(

Going out without going out

When not working, I waste my time on social media. Though social media most often gives the adrenaline rush, I still get bored. It feels surreal in a way that I see humans doing normal human things, but inside their homes while watching them on a small screen.

Now, I got up my bed, and trying to find something entertaining to do. Something, more free-feeling.. The only way I can think of is MINECRAFT!!!! I can still "go outside" without being outside. Though a bit different, it feels like I smel lthe fresh air out in nature. I get to explore and try to build stuff, without even dropping a sweat. Isn't that cool?

Well, I got to build a house, and a farm, with some few machines using redstone. It feels nice.. :)

Heaven on ears and hands??

Well, I got to go out without going out. Now, on the next level, how bout heaven without going out?

I don't mean dying okay? or reading the bible. I mean, listening to music..

Listening to music is indeed relaxing. some songs are so good, it feels out of earth. Songs like "Fireflies" by Owl City, "The Scientist" by Coldplay, and "Ave Maria" by Schubert, to name a few.

But you know what's better? Playing this music on guitar (a harp sounds more heavenlike but I don't have one :D). I've been learning some of these heavenly songs with my guitar. Some of them are difficult but I still try to learn them. I even forget the pain in my fingers as long as I hear that heavenly tune.

the world in different POV

When browsing through YouTube, it recommended me a video from one of my favorite channels: "Crash Course". The video is about free will. (here's the video)

Though I have finished the playlist like many years ago, after watching the video, it's like the way I saw the world got different. It got me asking questions again like "Do we really hae free will?", "Is everything that happened part of our fate?". Does that mean "COVID" was meant to happen?

Well, there's no one answer. but it lead me to rewatching the whole playlist, and got myself mindblown all over again. Some ideas just don't age. Our perspectives on these ideas change as we age and experience more.

After watching the playlist, I found a great philosophy channel by a professor in a university in US. He uploads some of his lectures on YouTube. (here's the channel: Jeffrey Kaplan) There's an interesting video about "ordinary people are evil". Eye-catching!!

After I watched the video, I really shouted "HOLY SH**T!!". Mind was blown again.
😈 I'm EVIIIL!!!

I got into the rabbit hole of philosophy. I read classic philosophy books like Plato's "Dialogues" and "Republic", Nietzsche's "Beyond Good & Evil", Camus "The Stranger" and more. Some are difficult reads, I had to have google/reddit by my side for someone to explain it further. I read novels which I read has some philosophical ideas embedded into it like Dostoevsky's "Crime and Punishment" and "The Brothers Karamazov", and Camus' "The Stranger".
All of them --- MIND-BLOWIIING!!!!!

Although it's not like me - I shared this to a workmate when working from office (at this point, We're already asked to work from office a few days per month). He was familiar with some concepts, but he recommended a TV show named "The Good Place". I watched the show, and.... I LOVE IT!!!! I am so invested with philosophy that thinking about all of those ideas had me on an existential crisis. It's official. My perspective on the world has changed. I don't even know what's real anymore.

I decided, I'll back off for a moment, and let ignorance be my bliss. Though I haven't really quit philosophy, I decided to distract my mind with other stuff

Cartoons are for kids??

Some days, I watch new stuff in Netflix. The comedy ones give me some distractions.

Now, that's when I got suggested a tv-series named "Bojack Horseman". I watched the first episode. It's funny, a talking horse, a worlf of anthophomorfized animals and witty jokes and lines.
Several episodes in... it got darker and sadder. It became so real that even the jokes can make me laugh, I still end up speechless.

One episode where his friend doesn't forgive him for leaving him after coming out of the closet and ended up with cancer got so real, I don't think a cartoon has ever done this before.
Another episode shows 'Princess Caroline' after a hard day and dealing with a difficult horse, didn't realize it's her birthday until her phone reminder her.
Another episode shows Bojack's deep overthinking thoughts even when his 'daughter' just asked her to buy milk. There's more of this.. lots of it.

I've watched cartoons like "Family Guy" or "Happy Tree Friends" which are clearly for adults, but not this kind of show. It's too real.. My older relatives often told me cartoons are for children. Well, I say to them, "THAT IS SO NOT TRUE!!".

The New Normal

The whole time, I'm writing this blogpost, It made me remember everything I did to get over the lockdown. Right now, we're just told to return to office 3 days a week. Maybe, it's over?
... well, i hope so. but the past 2 years is whole experience. I got to discover myself, feel heaven, and understand different perspectives. Even if the world is going back to normal, I'll never forget the stuff I get to experience while locked out of my own home.

Social media and news often says that right now, we're in the "new normal". Working from Home, Social Media trends, and more..
But I say, the new normal is not just the way we live has changed. It's also about how we see the world changed from that once-in-a-lifetime experience..

I just hope this will only happen once in anyone's lifetime..
