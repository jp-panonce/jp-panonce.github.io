High School Graduation

I graduated high school this morning. it was a long day, and i'm tired. I'm from a big public local high school. If you know someone from our city, chances are they're been to that school. There were hundreds of us, and everyone was waiting for everyone to go up the stage and receive their diplomas, get some awards, and say goodbyes. I'm one of them but it's just so exhausting. After the graduation song and the goodbyes, and the family dinner, I finally had a moment to reflect on the day.

I thought ...

All I did was wait, and wait, get in line, go up the stage, receive my diploma, handshake, waaaaaaaaaaiiit, sing, goodbye, and done. What an unproductive bullcrap day!!

Well, not everyone did the same. Those who got awards, or got into curriculars or clubs went to stage more than once. They were able to do some enterataining presentations, got more medals, and got more recognition. I was just an average student, and I didn't do anything special. Thus, I had to wait for everyone to do their thing.

Honestly, I hate waiting. I may not look like it, but i'm impatient. I don't want that anymore.

What I'll do next

I decided. I want to be better. College is my chance. I'll be more productive. I won't wait anymore. I will do better.

Throughout my college life, I'll make sure to become more than average. I'll give more effort. I'm willing to sacrifice my social life. At the end of it all, hopefully, I don't have to wait anymore.



You'll see...
