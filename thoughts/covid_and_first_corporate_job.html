my first corporate job aaand... covid happened

content
The Job Search Accepted one :D Covid happened

The Job Search

Three months since I got my professional license, I still have no plans on finding a new job. I'm staying at home, having the best time of my life, and enjoying the freedom I've yearned for since I started school. I was watching anime, play games, watch youtube, and more. I was an umemployed freeloader at our house. :D

But then, after a drunken night, and mysteriously waking up at home. I was supposed to come to a trip but I woke up late. I tried to find my phone to let my friends know I'm coming. But I can't find my phone anywhere. I was panicking. Throughout the day, I was trying to retrace my steps from last night. Alas! I can't find it. The realization dawned on me. I lost it. My graduation gift. I've had it for a year.
It was expensive as hell. And I just lost it due to carelessness. To atone for my mistake, I decided, IT IS TIME!!

Accepted One :D

I started looking for a job. I created a resume, and started sending applications anywhere I could. I got a couple of calls and went to interviews. Most of them, I didn't get a call back. Whenever I did, I find the job not fitting for me, or the pay is not what I expected.
A week after I sent my first application, I got a call for an interview. It went great and I found the job fitting for me. I accepted it when the offer met my expectations. I started the next week.

Covid happened

The news was everywhere. Usually, it's just other countries reporting a large number of cases. Of course, there was fear on my mind. I remember hospitals getting full in China and some cities there on lockdown. I've been a week on to my job when news came that a lockdown will happen starting March 16, 2020. When I asked my boss about it, he said that a WFH arrangement will take place until lockdown is lifted. Good thing, I just needed my laptop and a few hardware components to do my work. I thought it's just a few weeks, but lockdown turned to months. We were not supposed to go out unless for essential stuff. I was just working my ass off at home. But everywhere on the news, there was a growing number of cases. I didn't have to go out, since we have a lot of food stocked up. But the fear was there.

When I had to go out and buy grocery with mother, We wore those annoying face shields. We ready our quarantine passes and have to endure the long lines. The queue was long due to social distancing. A meter apart per person. When we get home, we disinfect everything we bought, and immediately take a good long bath. That was the new normal.

It's 2 months since the pandemic and my first day on job.
This is the year I got my first job, but also the first year we're locked out of our homes. My first pandemic but also had my first salary. I was happy to have a job, but I can't really 100% enjoy it knowing people are dying. I just hope this ends soon. I hope the world heals on this one.
